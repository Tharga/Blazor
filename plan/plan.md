## Decisions

- **Defaults do not move.** 63 call sites across 30 files in 8 repos. Absolute-primary and
  auto-refresh are both opt-in; the filed audit-log defect is therefore *fixable* by consumers
  rather than fixed for them. Chosen deliberately over flipping the default to absolute.
- **Site-level language is a scoped service, not a cascading value.** The consumer runs
  multi-tenant sites and needs language per tenant. A scoped service reads whatever tenant
  context the host already has, and reaches every render tree including Radzen dialogs — a
  cascading value placed around `@Body` would silently miss the 4 consuming files that are
  dialogs.
- **No `Tharga.Toolkit` change.** Keeps this a single-repo feature and avoids a release
  dependency chain.
